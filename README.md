# 🥤 Soft Drink Sales Dashboard

## 📊 Overview

The Soft Drink Sales Dashboard is an interactive Business Intelligence project built using Microsoft Power BI. It provides valuable insights into sales performance, revenue generation, profitability, and product trends across different companies and categories.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful visualizations, enabling users to monitor business performance and make data-driven decisions.

---

## 🚀 Features

* Revenue, Profit %, and Units Sold KPIs
* Revenue Growth Tracking
* Revenue Target Achievement KPI
* Units Sold Target Achievement KPI
* Year-wise Revenue Trend Analysis
* Company-wise Revenue Comparison
* Category-wise Revenue Distribution
* Product Performance Analysis
* Interactive Filters for Company, State, and Year

---

## 📈 Dashboard Visuals

* KPI Cards

  * Total Revenue Generated
  * Total Goods Sold
  * Profit Percentage

* KPI Visuals

  * Revenue Growth KPI
  * Units Sold KPI

* Charts

  * Revenue Growth Over Years
  * Revenue by Company
  * Revenue by Category
  * Revenue by Product

* Slicers

  * Company
  * Customer State
  * Year
